# Code of Conduct

SGBusBar is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person, and make it specific and constructive.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels and points of view.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through [github.com/syazfraser](https://github.com/syazfraser). Reports are kept confidential.

This code draws on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
